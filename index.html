Yield Forecasting & Crop Modelling

01

Beyond Rainfall Totals: Excess Moisture, Planting Window Shifts, and Provincial Yield Differences in Zambian Maize Systems

Using a 24-year panel across Zambia's ten provinces, this study shows that total seasonal rainfall barely explains maize yield variation on its own. A two-way fixed effects model finds that excess moisture, not drought, is the bigger constraint in most provinces, with rainfall efficiency also varying sharply by region, pointing to soil and management factors as key drivers alongside climate.
