About This Portfolio

This portfolio presents research and analytical work on decision-making under uncertainty, with applications in production systems and commodity markets. Current work extends these methods into optimisation, simulation and computational approaches.

Climate & Production Risk

01

Beyond Rainfall Totals: Excess Moisture, Planting Window Shifts, and Provincial Yield Differences in Zambian Maize Systems

Panel regression with SPI drought indexing shows that total seasonal rainfall explains very little of maize yield variation across Zambia’s provinces. Excess moisture, not drought, is the larger constraint in most provinces.
