About This Portfolio

This portfolio presents research and analytical work on decision-making under uncertainty, with applications in production systems and commodity markets. The work combines agricultural and economic data with statistical, econometric and quantitative methods to investigate production outcomes, market behaviour and risk. Current work extends these methods into optimisation, simulation and computational approaches.

Climate & Production Risk

02

Beyond Rainfall Totals: Excess Moisture, Planting Window Shifts, and Provincial Yield Differences in Zambian Maize Systems

Panel regression with SPI drought indexing shows that total seasonal rainfall explains very little of maize yield variation across Zambia’s provinces. Excess moisture, not drought, is the larger constraint in most provinces.
